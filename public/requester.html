DID YOU KNOW?

Know Your Blood Group

Understanding blood group compatibility can help you make informed decisions when requesting or donating blood.

O-

Universal Red Cell Donor

O-negative red blood cells can be donated to people of all blood groups.

AB+

Universal Red Cell Recipient

AB-positive individuals can receive red blood cells from all blood groups.

❤️ A Little Blood Donation Fact

One blood donation can help save multiple lives because donated blood can be separated into different components, such as red blood cells, plasma and platelets.

Note: This chart describes red blood cell compatibility. Actual transfusions require blood grouping, crossmatching and approval by qualified medical professionals.
